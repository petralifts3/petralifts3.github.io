About Us

  • Established in 1989, Petra Elevators Company is the exclusive and authorized distributor of KONE Elevators in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. For over three decades, Petra has built a strong reputation for delivering reliable, high-quality vertical transportation solutions tailored to the local market.

  • With a team of over 100 KONE-certified and factory-trained professionals, our engineers and technicians bring extensive hands-on experience across installation, modernization, and maintenance. Our operations are supported by the latest technologies, advanced communication systems, and a readily available stock of spare parts—ensuring prompt service, reduced downtime, and 24/7 after-sales support.

All equipment and services provided by Petra Elevators comply with the latest international codes and safety regulations, reflecting our unwavering commitment to quality, performance, and long-term customer trust.
